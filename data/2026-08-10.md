Result: 成功构造了一族有限秩李代数胚，其锚映射恰好实现由在原点消失到预设阶数的向量场生成的奇异叶状结构。

Conclusion: 这项工作建立了左对称代数胚与奇异叶状结构之间的新联系，为奇异叶状结构的有限秩表示提供了系统性构造方法。

Abstract: We construct a family of finite-rank Lie algebroids realizing, through their anchor maps, the singular foliations of vector fields vanishing to a prescribed order at the origin. The construction is obtained from the sub-adjacent Lie algebroids of a class of left-symmetric algebroids.
