We prove that a full $t$-star maximizes $co_p$ among $t$-intersecting families for every real $p\geq2$ throughout the sharp classical range $n\geq(t+1)(k-t+1)$, and we determine all equality cases. Using Bey's size-sensitive quadratic inequality, we extend the same framework to every nontrivial degree level: if $\mathcal{F}$ is intersecting, $n\geq2k$, and $1\leq r\leq k-1$, then a full point-star maximizes $\ell_{r,p}(\mathcal{F})$ for every real $p\geq2$, again with a complete equality classification. Thus the codegree theorem extends the sharp Wu--Zhang quadratic bound to every real $p\geq2$, completes the quadratic boundary equality classification, contains the Brooks--Linz conjecture as its $p=2$ special case, and, for integer exponents $p\geq2$, resolves the problem of Zhou--Yuan throughout the sharp Erdős--Ko--Rado range.
